Built as a portfolio template from my Travel and Tourism Management training, the same structure I'd apply to a real executive's trip.
The problem

Trips booked reactively, one confirmation at a time, spread across the inbox. Easy to miss a tight connection or notice a missing document two days before departure.

The solution

One tracker per trip: flights, ground transport, hotel confirmations, time-zone conversions, per-diem notes, and a document checklist, cross-checked against the calendar before booking.

The flow, start to finish

  • Pre-trip: confirm dates against the calendar, flag conflicts before booking
  • Booking: flights and hotels logged with confirmation numbers, cancellation windows, and cost
  • Documentation: passport, visa, and insurance checked against departure date
  • Day-of: one itinerary view with local time zones and ground transport times
  • Post-trip: expenses logged and reconciled against the trip budget

Problem

Solution

Result

Problem

A new e-commerce line had no documented process to run on, and no way to check whether partners were over- or under-audited on their expense cards.

Solution
